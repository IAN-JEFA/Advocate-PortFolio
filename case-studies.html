Contract Dispute Settlement

Real Estate

Land Title Dispute

Challenge: A decade-long ownership dispute over agricultural land in Kisumu, with conflicting title deeds.

Strategy: Conducted a full chain-of-title investigation and petitioned the Environment and Land Court.

Outcome: Court ruled in the client's favor, confirming full ownership rights.

4 months to resolution
Constitutional

Judicial Review Petition

Strategy: Filed an urgent judicial review petition citing procedural violations.

Outcome: High Court issued a unanimous ruling overturning the directive.

Unanimous ruling in client's favor

Real Estate

Commercial Lease Renegotiation

Challenge: A retail tenant faced an unfavorable lease renewal with steep rent escalation clauses.

Strategy: Renegotiated terms directly with the landlord's counsel, citing comparable market rates.

Outcome: Secured renewal at a fixed escalation cap over five years.

18% reduction in proposed rent
