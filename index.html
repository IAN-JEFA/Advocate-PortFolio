About the Chambers

Fifteen years arguing
the record straight.

Advocate John Mwangi, LLB, LLM

With over fifteen years of practice in Kenyan courts, Advocate John Mwangi leads Nairobi Legal Chambers with a commitment to justice and client-focused representation. Admitted to the Kenyan Bar in 2008, he specializes in civil litigation, corporate law, and constitutional matters — having argued landmark cases before the High Court and Court of Appeal.

The chambers' philosophy is simple: prepare rigorously, communicate plainly, and let the strength of the argument — not theatrics — carry the case.

Practice Areas

Civil Litigation

Representation in civil disputes, contract disagreements, and personal injury claims.

From pre-trial negotiation to appeal, we manage the full lifecycle of a civil claim — pleadings, discovery, and courtroom advocacy before the High Court.

Corporate Law

Advisory for businesses — formation, compliance, and governance.

Real Estate Law
