About Us

Fifteen years arguing
the record straight.

Nairobi Legal Chambers was founded in 2008 by Advocate John Mwangi following his admission to the Kenyan Bar. What began as a sole practice focused on civil litigation has grown into a chambers of five advocates handling corporate, constitutional, and real estate matters across Kenya.

The chambers' philosophy has stayed constant: prepare rigorously, communicate plainly, and let the strength of the argument — not theatrics — carry the case.
